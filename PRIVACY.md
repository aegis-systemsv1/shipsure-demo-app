# Harbour Walks privacy policy

Harbour Walks is a fictional app made for a ShipSure demonstration.

- **What we collect:** the email address you sign in with and the trails you save.
- **Why:** to keep your saved trails on your devices.
- **Who we share it with:** nobody. We do not sell personal information.
- **Access and correction:** email privacy@harbourwalks.example to see, correct or delete your data.
- **Complaints:** contact us first; you can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).
